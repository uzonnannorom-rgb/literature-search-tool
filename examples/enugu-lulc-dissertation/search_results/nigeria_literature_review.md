## Suggested narrative synthesis

The harvested literature suggests that Nigeria-focused urban growth research is concentrated in major metropolitan regions, especially Lagos and Abuja, with strong attention to built-up expansion, vegetation loss, conversion of peri-urban land, and planning consequences of rapid city growth. A recurring methodological pattern is multi-temporal image comparison for change detection, often framed as LULC transitions and in some cases linked to flood vulnerability, informal expansion, or environmental management. Sentinel-2-specific studies appear alongside a broader body of work using Landsat or mixed-sensor archives.
